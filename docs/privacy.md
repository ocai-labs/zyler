# Local notes and connected features

This guide explains the product's data boundaries. For the legal privacy policy,
use the **Privacy** link on the [Zyler website](https://zyler.app).

## Local notes

Zyler stores notes and attachments in your chosen [vault folder](local-vaults.md).
Creating, editing, and navigating local notes do not require an account.

Your vault is a normal folder on your computer. Local vault files are not encrypted
just because they belong to Zyler. Your operating system, disk encryption, file
permissions, backup tools, and any software you allow to read the folder determine
who can access those files.

## Built-in AI

Built-in AI uses a Zyler account and online services. When you ask AI to work with
notes, relevant prompts and note content may be sent through Zyler's AI service to
model providers to answer your request. Use these features only with content you
are comfortable processing through the service.

## Sync

Sync is an optional paid service associated with a Zyler account. Review its setup,
device-pairing and recovery instructions in the app before enabling it. Check the app
and website for current availability, pricing, and terms.

## Support and public feedback

GitHub Issues and pull requests are public. Remove personal notes, account identifiers,
tokens, and private file paths from screenshots, recordings, and logs before posting.
For account or billing matters, use [email support](../SUPPORT.md).
For vulnerabilities, follow the [security policy](../SECURITY.md).
