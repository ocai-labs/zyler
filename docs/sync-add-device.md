# Add a device

A new device joins your Sync in one of two ways: a device you already use approves it by
comparing a 6-digit code, or you enter your [recovery key](sync-recovery-key.md). You need
Sync turned on first; see [turn on Sync](sync-set-up.md).

Keep a device you already use open and nearby before you start. Its approval request lasts
10 minutes.

## On a computer

1. Install Zyler and open it. In the welcome window, click **Open vault from Sync**.
2. Your browser opens. Sign in with the same account you use for Sync, then return to Zyler.
   If the browser did not open, click **Copy sign-in link** and paste it into your browser.
3. Zyler shows **Connect this device** and waits for an existing device. A 6-digit code
   appears, such as `482 719`.
4. On a device you already use, approve the request (see below).
5. When the codes match on both devices, click **Codes match** on the new device.
6. Under **Choose vaults to download**, pick the vaults you want. Each becomes a folder on
   this device, saved in **Documents** unless you choose another place.
7. Click **Download**. Zyler opens your vault when it is ready.

## On iPhone or iPad

1. Install Zyler and open it. Tap **Open vault from Sync**.
2. Tap **Sign In** and sign in with your Sync account.
3. On **Connect This Device**, compare the code with the one on a device you already use, and
   tap **Codes Match** when they are the same.
4. Choose the vaults to download, then tap **Download**.

## Approve on a device you already use

On a computer, a dialog titled **A new device asks to join** opens by itself. Check that its
code is the same as the one on the new device, then click **Codes match**. If the codes differ,
or you did not start this, click **Deny**.

If you closed the dialog, or you are approving from an iPhone or iPad, open
**Settings → Sync**. A card reads **A new device asks to join**; tap **Review**.

## No other device at hand

On the new device, choose **Use your recovery key** (**Use Recovery Key** on iPhone and iPad)
instead of waiting for an approval. Enter the key and continue. Spaces, hyphens, and letter
case do not matter.

## Device limit

Every plan allows 20 devices. If you reach it, the new device shows **20 devices already**.
On a connected device, open **Settings → Sync**, find the device you no longer use under
**Devices**, and click **Remove**. Removed devices do not count toward the limit.
