# Plans and stopping Sync

## Plans

| Plan | Storage | Devices | Largest attachment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 1 GB | up to 20 | 5 MB |
| Plus | 10 GB | up to 20 | 200 MB |

You can sync as many vaults as you like; they share your plan's storage. Prices and current
terms are on the [pricing page](https://zyler.app/pricing). Buy or change a plan on your
account at [account.zyler.app](https://account.zyler.app), or inside the iPhone and iPad apps.
A plan bought in the App Store is managed on your iPhone or iPad.

## When you reach a limit

- **Storage full.** New changes wait on the device until there is room. Free up space or
  upgrade, and they upload.
- **An attachment is too large for the plan.** Other devices show the file as
  **On another device** until you upgrade.
- **20 devices.** Remove one you no longer use; see [add a device](sync-add-device.md#device-limit).

## Pause or turn off Sync for a vault

Turn off **Sync this vault** in **Settings → Sync**, or choose **Pause sync** from a page's
**…** menu. The vault stops syncing on this device only. Its cloud copy and your other devices
are not affected, and you can turn it back on later.

## Remove a device

On another connected device, open **Settings → Sync**, find it under **Devices**, and click
**Remove**. The removed device loses access to Sync and is signed out. Files already on it
stay there; they cannot be deleted remotely.

## Delete a vault from the cloud

In **Settings → Sync**, open the vault's **⋯** menu and choose **Delete from cloud…**. Type the
vault's name to confirm. Its cloud copy and cloud version history are deleted, and it stops
syncing on every device. Its folder stays on each device.

## If your subscription ends

1. Sync becomes download only for 30 days. Devices can still download, but nothing uploads,
   and **Settings → Sync** offers **Resubscribe**.
2. Renew within those 30 days to keep your cloud copies and sync again.
3. After 30 days, your cloud copies are deleted.

The vault folders on your devices are never touched.

## Sign out or delete your account

**Settings → General → Sign out** removes this device's Sync keys. To sync again after signing
back in, connect the device as in [add a device](sync-add-device.md). Your local files stay.

To delete your account, click **Manage ↗** in **Settings → General**, then **Delete account**.
A plan bought on the web is cancelled. A plan bought in the App Store must also be cancelled
in your Apple account's subscriptions.
