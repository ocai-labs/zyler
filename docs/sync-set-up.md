# Turn on Sync

Do this once, on the device that already has the vault you want to sync. To bring that vault
to more devices afterwards, see [add a device](sync-add-device.md).

## 1. Sign in

1. Open **Settings → General**. The first card is **Zyler account**.
2. Click **Sign in**. Your browser opens the Zyler sign-in page.
3. Choose **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Apple**, or **Continue with email**.
   With email, open the link we send you. It shows a 6-digit code; enter it on the
   **Check your email** page.
4. Return to Zyler. The card now shows your account.

## 2. Choose a plan

1. Open **Settings → Sync**.
2. Next to **Sync this vault**, click **Choose plan ↗**. Your account page opens in the browser.
3. Pick **Monthly** or **Yearly**, then **Choose plan** under Standard or Plus, and pay.

On iPhone and iPad you can subscribe in the app instead: **Settings → Sync → Upgrade**, then
**Subscribe**. See [plans and stopping Sync](sync-plans.md) for what each plan includes.

## 3. Sync the vault

1. In **Settings → Sync**, turn on **Sync this vault**.
2. The first time, Zyler creates your recovery key and shows **Save your recovery key**.
   Nothing uploads until you have saved it.
3. Copy the key into a password manager, or write it down and keep it away from your devices.
4. Tick **I saved it somewhere safe** and click **Start syncing**.

The **Status** row shows **Syncing…**, then **Up to date**. The vault's icon in the sidebar's
vault switcher gets a cloud mark.

If you click **Cancel** instead of saving the key, Sync stays off for the vault. Turn it on
again when you are ready to save the key.

## Sync more vaults

Open the other vault and turn on **Sync this vault** in its **Settings → Sync**. All your
vaults share one plan and one recovery key, so you will not be asked to save a key again.
